I am broadly interested in building low-latency systems: improving software performance by co-designing with application semantics and hardware.

My recent work focuses on software-defined memory systems to improve datacenter resource efficiency.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Brown University, advised by Malte Schwarzkopf.

Also, I was student researcher at SystemResearch@Google.
